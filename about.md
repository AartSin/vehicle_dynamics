# About

I'm a second-year Motorsport Engineering student at Coventry University.

I got into vehicle dynamics through the course and started writing my own models to understand it properly: first the textbook bicycle and quarter-car models, then a basic lap time simulator, and now a four-wheel version with a proper powertrain.

Things I use: MATLAB and Simulink, Excel for car data, and Obsidian for notes.

The best way to contact me is through the links at the bottom of the page.
