# About

I'm a second-year Motorsport Engineering student at Coventry University.

I got into cars by watching too much Initial D. That turned into a lot of hours on Assetto Corsa Competizione, where I started building my own setups from MoTeC data and Milliken's Race Car Vehicle Dynamics instead of copying other people's. That's where I first wanted to know why a car does what it does.

My favourite drivers are Senna on four wheels and Casey Stoner on two.

On the course I got into vehicle dynamics properly and started writing my own models to understand it: first the textbook bicycle and quarter-car models, then a basic lap time simulator, and now a four-wheel version with a proper powertrain.

I'm also on the Coventry University Formula Student team, where I work in performance and simulation engineering.

Tools I use: MATLAB and Simulink, ADAMS/Car, OptimumLap, Assetto Corsa and ACC with MoTeC, CATIA, SolidWorks and Excel.

I'm looking for a 2027–28 placement year (flexible on start date) in performance engineering, vehicle dynamics or VD simulation, chassis, or trackside engineering.
