A001 is my first lap-time simulator. It is a point-mass, quasi-steady model that turns a car's grip, power and aero into a speed profile and a lap time. It answers first-order questions for a Formula Student car: what a unit of downforce is worth in lap time, and whether the car is grip-limited or power-limited.

I matched OptimumLap's simplifications on purpose. With the same physics on both sides, any difference comes from my code, so OptimumLap works as a test bench for the solver.

## Key results

1. **Matches OptimumLap to 0.6%** on the Coventry FSUK car: 59.35 s against 58.98 s, top speed 109.3 against 110.6 km/h, minimum speed 31.3 against 31.2 km/h.
2. **The comparison found five bugs.** The two largest: braking ignored the tyre limit (1.7 s), and rear-wheel drive cut engine power instead of drive grip (1.0 s; top speed 88 → 109 km/h).
3. **Each +1 ClA saves about 1.3 s** on FSEC 2025. The car is grip-limited: +5% lateral grip is worth 1.32 s, +5% engine torque is worth nothing.
4. **Known limits:** curvature noise gives ±0.5% (±0.3 s) numerical uncertainty, and drive grip is fixed at 45% of tyre grip to copy OptimumLap's 2WD.
