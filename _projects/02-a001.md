The car is a point mass. The solver does a forward pass (accelerate as hard as the engine and tyres allow) and a backward pass (brake as late as the tyres allow), and the lap is the lower of the two speeds at each point on the track.

Car data comes from Excel sheets: aero map, engine map, gearbox, brakes, and either a simple tyre or a Magic Formula tyre. It also has weight transfer and a g-g loop.


A point mass gives a lap time quickly but says nothing about balance in a corner, which is why the later versions move to a bicycle and then a four-wheel model. I'm not reusing this solver in C001.
