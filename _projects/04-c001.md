Four wheels, nonlinear tyres and load transfer. One vehicle model builds two maps: MMM for balance, GGV for performance.

## Vehicle model

| Part | Model |
|---|---|
| Tyres | Magic Formula, combined slip |
| Load transfer | Quasi-static, longitudinal and lateral |
| Aero | Constant ClA, CdA |
| Drivetrain | Rear-wheel drive, limited-slip differential |
| Brakes | Pedal to disc torque, brake balance |

## MMM: balance

Speed, steer δ, sideslip β and drive/brake fixed; `fsolve` finds the steady state:

$$
\sum F_x = m\,a_x, \qquad \sum F_y = m\,a_y, \qquad T_{wheel,i} - T_{b,i} - F_{x,i}R_{e,i} = 0
$$

$$
C_N = \frac{\sum M_z}{m\,g\,L} \ \text{ against } a_y
$$

## GGV: performance envelope

At each speed and a<sub>y</sub>, `fmincon` finds the limits of a<sub>x</sub> in yaw balance:

$$
a_{x,\max/\min}(a_y, V) \quad \text{subject to the equilibrium above and } C_N = 0
$$
