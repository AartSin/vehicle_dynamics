For a given speed and lateral acceleration, find the sideslip and steer angle that balance the car, along with the load and force at each tyre. Sweeping that over speed and g gives the GGV envelope, which a new speed-profile solver will then use.

Done so far:

- lateral and longitudinal weight transfer
- slip angle kinematics at each wheel
- the tyre model pieces

Still to do:

- the equilibrium solver for one cornering point
- the speed and g sweep that builds the GGV
- a lap solver that uses it
